The resonance cascade has opened Sector C. Hold the test chamber and keep the reactor's coolant line intact while wave after wave comes down the four approaches — the south access, the observation window, the storage vent, and the service tunnel once the blast doors give.

Nothing here is a file. The level is brushes, two corners and a material id each; the textures are short programs over a shared operator library; the lighting is a radiosity solve run in the browser at load, every lamp shadow-traced then bounced, stored in Valve's three-vector basis. Enemies are hinge trees of generated solids — a chamfered box, a capsule, a dome, a cylinder, built at load from a few operators over a solid, so every edge has something for a highlight to run along. They are lit by the same shadow tracer the lightmap used, and they path over a surface lattice solved from the geometry itself. Every gunshot, groan and explosion is synthesised on the spot. Press T to peel the render channels apart, V for the build readout.
